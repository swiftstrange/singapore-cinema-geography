# Stage 1E reconciliation and verification-priority report

## Scope

All 104 non-core records in the Stage 1D master inventory were reviewed. This is a planning layer: no row was added to or changed in operations.csv or sites.csv, and no geocoding was attempted.

## Method

Each candidate has six transparent heuristic dimensions in verification_priority.csv: source strength (1-3), temporal, geographic, typology, lineage and narrative value (each 0-2). The summed score supports—not replaces—research judgement. Priority A is selected by combined analytical value and balanced shortlist composition, rather than a score cutoff alone; a broad-list-only row can be Priority A where resolving it would materially clarify an important gap or lineage.

## Dataset gaps

### Temporal

The verified core is most secure for selected 1930s, 1960s, 1970s, 1990s and 2010s-present examples. It is weakest for pre-1930, 1930-49 beyond prominent central cinemas, 1950-69 outside Queenstown/Geylang examples, and the 2000-09 transition from neighbourhood cinema to mall multiplex. The Priority A list intentionally includes Da Dong Ya, the amusement-park predecessors, and multiple 1990s-2020s handovers.

### Geographic

The historic central area is comparatively strong. The most important gaps are north (Woodlands, Sembawang and Yishun), north-east (Punggol/Sengkang/Hougang), west (Clementi, Bukit Batok, Jurong), and eastern coastal/new-town areas (Bedok, Pasir Ris, Marine Parade and Katong). Several high-priority candidates improve these without treating the broad-list area labels as addresses.

### Venue type

Purpose-built/standalone historic theatres and mall-integrated multiplexes have the clearest starting evidence. The weakest categories are neighbourhood commercial-centre cinemas, amusement-park/entertainment-complex cinemas, drive-in/recreational venues, and mixed-use redevelopment continuities. Priority A targets those distinctions before any quantitative comparison.

### Incomplete lineages

Priority review identifies Balestier (Hoover/President to later cinema), Great World, New World/City Square, Golden Mile Tower, Clementi (Empress/321), Bedok (Princess Raja/Djitsun), Ang Mo Kio, Lido, Toa Payoh, Shaw Tower, and the major mall operator handovers as the main incomplete clusters. The single likely duplicate is the ambiguous Jubilee identity, which could refer to different locations and periods.

## Recommended verified-dataset size

Aim for **about 70-90 well-evidenced operation records at roughly 55-70 physical sites**, rather than trying to certify every discovery row. This would retain enough operations for temporal and spatial analysis while allowing each record to meet the project’s address, chronology, venue-context and source-traceability standards.
