# Stage 1H evidence expansion and consolidation report

## Core change

| Measure | Before | After |
|---|---:|---:|
| Verified operations | 38 | 42 |
| Physical sites | 32 | 35 |

New operations: Hoover Theatre (1960-1996); President Theatre (1973-1996); Shaw Theatres Waterway Point (opened 21 January 2016); and Shaw Theatres JCube (opened April 2012). No new site was geocoded.

Hoover and President are preserved as distinct operations at SGC-S-033, 360 Balestier Road. NHB identifies the later Shaw Plaza complex as occupying their former site; this is a same-site lineage, not a merger of their operation records. Waterway Point and JCube are preserved as actual Shaw cinema-operation names at separate mall sites.

The partially supported group remains Causeway Point, Century Square, Cineleisure, Downtown East, JEM, West Mall, Seletar Mall, Leisure Park Kallang and 321 Clementi. The remaining gaps are principally full operational addresses, inception chronology, and clearly bounded operator-tenure sequences. These were not forced into the core.

## Second source-first harvest

Three additional sources were registered: a CNA report on the JEM closure and linked Cathay estate-wide closure sequence; a URA heritage guide on Great World/New World/Gay World; and Shaw's JCube opening material. The harvest now contains **33 evidence rows** supporting **23 unique candidates**.

The new URA material strengthens site-level documentation for Great World and New World. It does not identify individual cinema operations well enough to convert those complexes into operation records. The second CNA source corroborates closure and operator-transition facts across JEM, West Mall, Cineleisure, Causeway Point and Century Square. Shaw's material provides a direct official JCube opening source.

## Collateral Priority B evidence

Evidence was captured for Empress, Bedok, Jubilee, Savoy, Princess/Raja, Galaxy, Jurong Drive-In, Lido and other linked candidates where source scope permitted. None is marked evidence-ready for formal Priority B verification yet: each still lacks at least one required core element, normally a precise operational address or defensible closing/lineage chronology.

## Clusters improved

- **Balestier:** strongest improvement; Hoover/President/Shaw Plaza relationship now explicit.
- **North-east/west multiplexes:** Waterway Point, JCube and Seletar have official Shaw chronology.
- **Mall operator handovers:** Cathay closure sequence is now better corroborated across multiple sites.
- **Amusement parks:** Great World and New World have Tier 1 site/redevelopment context but insufficient individual-theatre separation.

## Remaining bottlenecks

Great World/New World individual cinema identities; Bedok and Clementi full address/tenure chains; Golden Mile historical operations; Da Dong Ya and early standalone cinemas; and precise opening histories for several 1990s-2010s mall handovers remain unresolved. Cinema Treasures pagination is still not extractable in the research surface, and broad-list claims remain discovery leads only.
