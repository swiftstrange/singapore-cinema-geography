## Entity-resolution findings

- Golden Mile Tower is a building/site label. The relevant Priority A cinema operation, The Projector at Golden Mile Tower, is already SGC-O-015 at SGC-S-011; no duplicate was created.
- Great World Amusement Park and New World Amusement Park are site/complex labels. Their individual historical theatre operations are not sufficiently separated by the available evidence, so they remain site-only research leads.
- The remaining mall-oriented labels—including Causeway Point, Century Square, Cineleisure, Djitsun Bedok, Downtown East, Funan, Jem, JCube, Waterway Point, West Mall, Seletar Mall and Leisure Park Kallang—were not converted into operations merely because a cinema tenancy is plausible. Their actual operation names, periods, addresses and operator transitions require Tier 1/2 documentation.

## Improved coverage and remaining limits

The additions improve 2000s–2010s mall-integrated coverage in Ang Mo Kio and Marine Parade, but do not resolve the older historical gaps, west/north/north-east coverage, or entertainment-complex lineages. Balestier (Hoover/President), Great World, New World/City Square, Clementi, Bedok, Ang Mo Kio and mall handovers remain the priority lineage research clusters.

The main evidence constraint is that the Stage 1D broad list supplies leads and some reported figures, but it does not itself establish the required operational identity and address chronology. No screens, seats or dates from that discovery list were copied into the core without a dated supporting source.
