# Stage 1G source-first evidence harvest report

## Scope

This was an evidence-only pass. No operation or site was added, changed or geocoded. The Stage 1F verification audit was updated with an evidence-status field for all 25 Priority A records; the current core remains 38 operations and 32 sites.

## Harvest result

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Authoritative or established sources harvested | 7 |
| Structured evidence rows | 24 |
| Unique candidate records supported | 20 |
| Priority A records previously insufficient and now verifiable | 4 |
| Priority A records partially supported | 9 |
| Priority A records still insufficient | 7 |

The 25-row status file also retains the two already-added Stage 1F cases, two site-only cases and one existing-core lineage case. Thus it reports six now-verifiable records overall, of which four are upgrades from the earlier insufficient-evidence group.

## Strongest multi-cinema sources

1. **NLB BiblioAsia, A Century of Singapore's Cinemas (SRC-001).** It provides a common chronology for post-1965 new-town cinema expansion and supports collateral candidates including Empress, Bedok, Jubilee, Savoy, Princess/Raja, Galaxy and Jurong Drive-In.
2. **NHB Roots Balestier Heritage Trail booklet (SRC-033).** It documents both Hoover and President, their periods, the New Hoover return, and continuity to Shaw Plaza at 360 Balestier Road.
3. **Shaw Theatres Waterway Point history page (SRC-034).** It directly documents Waterway Point’s full address, 21 January 2016 opening and 10 screens, while also supplying JCube’s April 2012 and Seletar’s December 2014 opening references.
4. **CNA operator/closure reporting (SRC-031, SRC-032, SRC-035, SRC-036).** These sources connect several Cathay and mall-cinema records in one set: Causeway Point, Downtown East, West Mall, JEM, Century Square, Cineleisure, 321 Clementi, Leisure Park Kallang and Seletar Mall.

## Priority A status changes

**Now verifiable at the next decision stage:** Hoover; President; Shaw Theatres Waterway Point; Shaw Theatres JCube. Their evidence includes a cinema identity and sufficient chronology/context to decide whether each warrants an operation record, subject to the normal core-field review.

**Partially supported:** Causeway Point; Century Square; Cineleisure; Downtown East; JEM; West Mall; Seletar Mall; Leisure Park Kallang; 321 Clementi. These have reliable operator, closure, transition or redevelopment evidence, but usually still lack a complete operation-period/address chain.

**Still insufficient:** Djitsun Bedok; Funan; Grand Great World City; i12 Katong; Sun Plaza; Bugis Junction; Da Dong Ya. Their broad-list leads were not treated as verified facts.

**Special cases:** Golden Mile Tower remains an existing-core site lineage; Great World Amusement Park and New World Amusement Park remain site-only complexes pending separable theatre-operation evidence.

## Clusters improved

- **Balestier:** substantially improved, with an authoritative Hoover/President/Shaw Plaza lineage.
- **North-east and west suburban multiplexes:** Waterway Point, JCube and Seletar now have an official operator-history source.
- **Cathay mall handovers and closure/reuse:** the 2024-25 reports provide shared context across multiple sites, though not all inception dates.
- **New towns and recreation:** BiblioAsia strengthens the evidence base for later Priority B work in Clementi, Bedok, Ang Mo Kio, Boon Lay, Geylang and Jurong.

## Remaining weak clusters and access limits

Great World/New World individual theatre identities, Da Dong Ya, early standalones, Djitsun Bedok and several mall opening lineages still require targeted institutional, archival or official-property material. NewspaperSG search results were used only to locate candidate publications; no search-result snippet was entered as evidence. Cinema Treasures remains an unextracted reconciliation source because full listing pagination was unavailable in the research surface.
